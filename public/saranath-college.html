“A Proud Legacy of Learning, Unity, and Academic Rigour Since 1882”

It is with immense pride that I welcome you to Saranath College, Kuliyapitiya. Founded in 1882 under Headmaster Anthony de Silva with just thirty students, our College has grown over more than a century into an esteemed fountain of knowledge shaping intellectuals, doctors, engineers, educators, and champions. Guided by our sacred motto “පඤ්ඤා නරානං රතනං”, we continue elevating Saranath College to greater heights alongside our dedicated staff, students, parents, and global alumni.

R.M.C. Randeniya
Principal · Saranath College, Kuliyapitiya
